Soul Bond Support

Need help with Soul Bond, or want to tell us what you think? Email hellopofigames@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

When something goes wrong, these details help us find it:

  • your phone or tablet model and its iOS or Android version;
  • the game version, shown on the main menu (for example v1.0.0);
  • the level number and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

How do I play?

One swipe moves both Ada and her cat Maysa the same way. When a wall stops one of them, the other keeps going. Bring each of them to her own goal within the move limit. The shortest solutions earn three stars.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Undo takes back your last move and Restart starts the level again. The Hint button shows the next move after a short ad. Every level can be solved without hints.

Why can't I get a hint?

Each hint comes with a short ad, so hints need an internet connection. When your device is offline, the game shows a "No Internet" message; connect and try again. If you're online but no ad is available, you get the hint anyway.

When does the game show ads?

When you ask for a hint, and now and then between levels. Never during a puzzle.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so deleting the game also deletes your progress. New Game on the main menu erases your progress after asking you first.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Everything except hints works without an internet connection.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Open Credits & Privacy from the main menu. There you can turn Share Play Data on or off and, where it applies, change your ad choices under Ad Privacy Options. The privacy policy explains what information is collected and how to ask us to delete it.
