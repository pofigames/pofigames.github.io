Soul Bond Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the game Soul Bond collects, why it is collected, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what choices and rights you have. It applies to every version of the game: iPhone and iPad, Android, and desktop (Windows, macOS and Linux).

Soul Bond is made and published by Pofi Games. Pofi Games is not a company. It is the name under which Ertuğrul Sırma, an individual game developer based in Türkiye, publishes games. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Ertuğrul Sırma, publishing as Pofi Games.

Contact: hellopofigames@gmail.com

The short version

  • Soul Bond has no accounts. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • To learn how players get on with the levels and to fix bugs, the game sends gameplay events (for example, "level 12 completed in 40 moves") and crash reports to Google Analytics. They are linked to a random ID, not to you. This happens only if you allow it when the game asks.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Android, the game shows ads from Google AdMob: a rewarded ad you can choose to watch to get a hint, and now and then a short ad after you complete a level. Google collects some information about your device and the ads you see in order to show and measure ads. Hints are optional: every level can be solved without them. The desktop versions have no ads.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • You can change your choices at any time in the Credits & Privacy screen (in the main menu), and you can contact us with any question or request.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Ertuğrul Sırma, publishing as Pofi Games, is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. This makes us the "controller" under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the "data controller" (veri sorumlusu) under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). You can reach us at hellopofigames@gmail.com.

Google provides our analytics service and processes that data on our behalf. For advertising, Google is an independent controller of the data it collects through AdMob (see section 3.3).

2. Information stored only on your device

The game saves the following on your device. It is not sent to us:

  • your progress: the levels you have completed, the stars you earned on each, the level you were playing and the tutorials you have finished;
  • your sound and music settings;
  • your privacy choices;
  • a small file the game uses to report crashes and unfinished levels on the next launch: the level being played, the last few game actions (such as "move" or "undo") and how many times you have started a new game;
  • on desktop only: the random analytics ID described in section 3.1 and any events still waiting to be sent.

Choosing New Game erases your progress. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this information.

3. Information sent from your device

3.1 Gameplay analytics

On iPhone, iPad and Android we use Google Analytics for Firebase. The desktop versions send the same events directly to Google Analytics. Analytics tells us which levels are too hard or too easy, where players get stuck, and whether the game runs well on different devices.

The following is sent:

  • Gameplay events: starting, finishing, restarting or leaving a level; the level number and result; the number of moves, undos and hints used; stars earned; time played; which attempt it was; finishing a tutorial or the whole game; how a rewarded ad went (watched, closed early or not available); and whether an ad was shown after a level (shown, skipped or not available).
  • A random ID: Google Analytics creates a random ID for this installation of the game (on desktop, the game creates it). The ID is not linked to your name, email address or any account, and it changes if you reinstall the game.
  • Device and app information, collected automatically by Google Analytics: device model and type, operating system and version, app version, language, when the game was first opened, and the number and length of play sessions.
  • Approximate location: Google Analytics uses your IP address to estimate your country, region and city. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.

The game does not let Google Analytics collect your device's advertising identifier, and we do not use analytics data to target ads.

3.2 Crash and error reports

If the game crashes or runs into an error, it sends a short technical report along with the analytics described above. The report holds the error message, the place in the game's code where it happened, the level being played and the last few game actions. It contains no personal information beyond the random ID in section 3.1.

3.3 Advertising (iPhone, iPad and Android only)

The game shows two kinds of ads from Google AdMob:

  • Hint ads: When you ask for a hint, the game offers a rewarded video ad. You get the hint after watching the ad.
  • Ads between levels: Now and then, after you complete a level and before the Level Complete screen, the game shows a full-screen ad (an "interstitial"). It shows at most one every few minutes of play, counting hint ads, never during a level, and never after the first four levels or the last one.

To have ads ready, the game loads one of each kind in advance when it starts (after the consent step described in section 6) and again after each ad it shows.

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the game automatically collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device and the game, such as device model, operating system, app version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, for example which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers. The game does not ask for tracking permission on iOS (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use the Android advertising ID. Google therefore cannot use an advertising identifier to tailor ads to you based on your activity in other apps and websites. Google may still use identifiers that are limited to our apps (such as the identifier for vendors on iOS or the app set ID on Android), for example to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, to personalize ads. Google is an independent controller of this information. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Tapping an ad may take you to another app or website, which has its own privacy policy.

3.4 Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you. Our email service is Gmail, provided by Google.

3.5 Information from app stores and Steam

Apple, Google and Valve (Steam) process information when you download, update and use the game, under their own privacy policies. If you have allowed it in your device or account settings, they may share aggregated statistics (such as download numbers) and crash reports with us. This information does not identify you to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or precise location. The game has no purchases, so we collect no payment information. We do not track your activity in other apps or on websites.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • run the game and keep your progress on your device;
  • understand how the levels are played and improve their difficulty, the tutorials and the hints;
  • find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • show ads, which fund the game;
  • answer your messages and requests;
  • comply with the law and protect against fraud and abuse.

We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Who we share information with

  • Google (analytics): Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Analytics, as our service provider (processor), under Google's data processing terms. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Google (advertising): Google AdMob, as an independent controller (see section 3.3).
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or when it is needed to protect the rights and safety of our players, ourselves or others.
  • A new publisher: if the game is ever transferred to another publisher, the information may be transferred with it. The new publisher will have to respect this policy or tell you about any changes.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else.

6. Your choices

  • Analytics question: The first time you open the game, it asks whether you allow gameplay analytics ("Share Play Data?"). Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing at all if you choose "Don't Allow".
  • Ad consent form: If you live in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland or another place where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your ad choices before any ad is loaded.
  • Changing your choices: In the Credits & Privacy screen (in the main menu) you can turn "Share Play Data" on or off, reopen Google's consent form where it applies ("Ad Privacy Options") and open this policy. A change applies from then on. It does not affect data collected before it. On desktop, turning analytics off also deletes the events still waiting to be sent.
  • Tracking on iOS: The game never asks to track you and does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Advertising ID on Android: The game does not use it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Hint ads: Hints are optional, and every level can be solved without them. The ads shown now and then between levels are part of the free mobile versions; the desktop versions have no ads.
  • Uninstalling: This deletes everything stored on your device and stops all collection. If you install the game again, it starts with new random IDs.
  • Contacting us: You can make any request by email (see section 8).

7. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you choose New Game (progress only) or uninstall the game.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics deletes player-level and event-level data automatically after 14 months. Aggregated reports, which do not identify any player, may be kept longer.
  • Advertising: Google keeps the data it collects through AdMob under its own retention policies.
  • Emails: as long as we need them to handle your request, and no longer than 2 years after our last exchange, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. Accessing or deleting your information

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or ask any question about it, at hellopofigames@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, or sooner where local law requires it. Requests are free.

The game has no accounts, so we do not know who you are, and we usually cannot link an email to the analytics records of a particular device. If you want your analytics data deleted, the simplest way is to turn "Share Play Data" off in the Credits & Privacy screen and then uninstall the game. No new data will be collected, and the remaining records will be deleted automatically after 14 months (see section 7). If you want them deleted sooner, contact us and we will do what we reasonably can. For data Google collects through AdMob, you can also use the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. International transfers

We are based in Türkiye. Google processes data on servers in many countries, including the United States. When data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Emails you send us are handled by us in Türkiye and stored by Gmail.

10. Security

Everything the game sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). There are no accounts or passwords. The data on your device is kept in the game's own app storage. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities where the law requires it.

11. Children

Soul Bond is a challenging puzzle game made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we can.

In some countries, for example some EU countries where the age is 16, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to agree to analytics or personalized ads if you are younger. If this applies to you, please ask them before you make a choice in the consent form.
